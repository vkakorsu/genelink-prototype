Scope on the facts entered: in scope

§Established in law

This configuration maps the commercial route only. Brazil does not split the application by declared purpose. Lei Art. 12 §2 gates on downstream acts and Decreto Art. 22 is one form, so the commercial premise decides which triggers occur rather than which form is filed. Benefit sharing attaches only at economic exploitation of a finished product. A purely academic user reaches the publication trigger and stops there.

§Established in lawLei Art. 2 I, VIII; Art. 16; Art. 17 §1

Patrimônio genético is informação de origem genética (Lei Art. 2 I) and access is research or technological development on that information (Art. 2 VIII), including provenance ex situ or in silico. Brazil sets scope by activity, never by whether a function was previously demonstrated or by whether the material was purchased. Art. 17 §1 names the manufacturer of the finished product as obligor irrespective of who did the research.

This is information about requirements that appear to apply on the facts entered. It is not legal advice and not an approval. The decision rests with the parties and their advisers. Everything shown here is recorded in what GENE-LINK told you.

Eligibility on the facts entered

§Established in lawLei Art. 12 II

A foreign company registers only associated with a Brazilian research institution. It can never register in its own name.

A Brazilian co-registrant record is required.

§Established in lawDecreto Arts. 27, 28, 29

Where the material is in the border strip, oceanic islands, jurisdictional waters, the continental shelf or the EEZ and a foreign nexus exists, prior assent from the Conselho de Defesa Nacional or the Comando da Marinha is required and the cadastro moves to awaiting assent.

Filling in the SisGen form is the request. SisGen notifies within 5 days, the authority has 60 days, suspended while information is outstanding. Assent is revocable and a change of ownership must be reported within 30 days.
